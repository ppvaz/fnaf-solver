# `apps/lab`

The lab running itself (ROADMAP S7): the Companion job queue, the night jobs it
holds, and the overnight window that plays them on the phone between 01:30 and
07:00 (Pedro, 2026-09-27: "Yes, play nights"). A night job runs only a committed
winner, by the runner its schema fixes; the window never promotes anything.

A plain folder, not a workspace (no `package.json`). The serial lease these
scripts hold is Play's (`packages/play/src/safety/companion_device_lock.py`);
each script puts that folder on its import path. The queue's state stays where
every checkout finds it, the main checkout's `captures/cue-helper/`, a stored
name.

`tools/device/overnight-window.py` stays as a thin forwarder while a host's
installed systemd units still name it (`lab.overnight-window-path` in
`legacy-paths.json`). After pulling, re-render the units once:
`python3 apps/lab/overnight-window.py units --serial ID --out ~/.config/systemd/user`
and `systemctl --user daemon-reload`.

## Scripts

Entry points and checks that lived in `tools/` until the ADR 0002 layout
moved them here, with the description their tool index gave them.

| Script | Kind | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `apps/lab/companion-queue.sh enqueue\|list\|cancel IDS --reason TEXT\|run [--wait SECONDS] [--interval SECONDS] [--max-jobs N] [--nights]` | device action/check | Persistent closed-vocabulary queue for `setup`, `menu-check`, `night-check`, and -- since Pedro's "Yes, play nights" (2026-09-27) -- `night`: `enqueue night --game fnaf2\|fnaf1\|fnaf4 --winner tools/device/X-winner.json --night N [--label L] [--audio]`, one night of a COMMITTED winner, validated at enqueue (custody, schema, the winner's own night, a fresh emit for FNaF 2) and bound by the winner's and the plan's sha256 and a budget (`night_jobs.py`). Only `run --nights` claims a night, and only the overnight window passes it: `cue.queue.run` drains setups and checks and reports `nights-held=N`. Enqueue/list work without a phone; run holds safely until exactly one ADB device is awake and unlocked. `--max-jobs 1` stops after one DONE job (`QUEUE PAUSED`), which is how the window checks the phone between jobs. A job runs in the runner's process group and the runner survives an interrupt to record it (a night interrupted or killed ends FAILED and is never replayed; a setup or check goes back to PENDING); a job past its ceiling gets SIGINT, SIGTERM, then SIGKILL on its whole process tree. Every job a window leaves pending carries its `windowNote`. `cancel` retires PENDING jobs by id and keeps each record (`CANCELLED`, `cancelledAt`, `cancelReason`); a job not PENDING, or an unknown id, refuses the whole call. It never auto-unlocks, taps the game, accepts shell text, or accepts coordinates/HID. |
| `apps/lab/companion-queue.py` | internal module | Persistent queue implementation and retryable absent-device HOLD state used by `companion-queue.sh`; exports `JOB_TIMEOUT_S` (360 s, a setup or check's ceiling), `job_timeout_s` (a night's `budgetS`), `make_night_job`, `note_pending` and `release_running` for the overnight window. The queue file is host-wide (`state_dir()/queued-jobs.json`). |
| `apps/lab/test/test-companion-queue.py` | check | No-device regression for queue persistence, stale-run recovery boundary, safe command vocabulary, absent-device HOLD, one-job runs, window notes, the release of a stopped job (never while a live runner owns the queue), and `cancel` (PENDING only, all-or-nothing, records kept). |
| `apps/lab/overnight-window.py [run\|preflight\|restore\|units] [--live --confirm-live] [--serial ID] [--start HH:MM] [--end HH:MM] [--battery-floor PCT] [--lock-wait S]` | **device action** (live only) | **Overnight windows (Pedro, 2026-09-27): the queue runs on his own phone overnight, on the charger.** The window defaults to **01:30-07:00 local** (`--start`/`--end` or `FNAF_WINDOW_START`/`FNAF_WINDOW_END`). It may be armed up to 4 h before it opens (`--max-arm-lead`), from which point it holds the phone awake. Without `--live --confirm-live` it is a dry run that prints the resolved window, its sha256, the deadline and the queue, and makes no adb call. A live window takes the serial lease. It first restores anything a killed window left behind, then refuses LEASE_BUSY, OUTSIDE_WINDOW, DEVICE_ABSENT, CAPABILITY (`capabilities.mjs`: target and Companion installed), LOCKED, IN_USE and POWER. LOCKED means the keyguard is showing or the screen is asleep, waited for up to `--lock-wait` (600 s) and never unlocked; the pending jobs get a queue note. IN_USE means a call is active, or the foreground is not the launcher, the Companion or a target game. POWER means unplugged, under 50 % (`--battery-floor`) or at 45 C or more. Next it records `stay_on_while_plugged_in`, screen timeout, brightness and its mode, `heads_up_notifications_enabled`, `zen_mode` and airplane mode, writes that record to disk, and sets stay-awake-while-charging (7), heads-up notifications off (0) and Do Not Disturb to priority (`cmd notification set_dnd priority`, zen_mode 1; Pedro, 2026-09-29, ADR 0002 decision 2), reading each back. If either cannot be set the window ends DND (exit 75) before any job, with everything it changed restored. A DND it finds already on (the owner's schedule) is left on and never turned off. It then runs the queue one job at a time, repeating the checks between jobs. No job starts that cannot finish before the deadline. At the deadline the queue child gets SIGINT, then SIGTERM, then SIGKILL, and the whole stop fits before the window end, measured on both the monotonic and the wall clock (a suspended host cannot overrun it). At the end, on any signal and on a job failure, the window returns a stopped job to PENDING, leaves the screen as it found it (stops capture it started, presses HOME only if it found the launcher and a game or the Companion is in front), restores every restored setting and reads each back (DND through `set_dnd off`, and only when the window found it off), then releases the lease. Airplane mode is only ever witnessed: a drift is reported, never written. Whether this handset's SystemUI honours the heads-up setting is not yet measured on the phone. Night jobs (Pedro, 2026-09-27: "Yes, play nights"): the window's queue child alone passes `--nights`; no job starts unless its own ceiling (a night's `budgetS`) fits before the stop instant, which also reserves the window's own title recovery; a night killed before it could observe the title is recovered by `night-job.py title --recover`; in the morning, after the lease, each night's run is packed (`npm run evidence -- pack`, if night-run.sh did not) or its FNaF 4 run record named, and one summary line per window is appended to `artifacts/overnight-windows/summary.log`. Telemetry: `artifacts/overnight-windows/<id>/window.json`, `events.jsonl` and `queue.log`. Exit 0 COMPLETE/DEADLINE, 75 a refusal (DND included), 1 JOB_FAILED, 130 ABORTED, 3 a setting not restored. The serial is `--serial`, else `FNAF_SERIAL` or `ANDROID_SERIAL`, else the local profile. `preflight [--json]` makes the same reads and says FIT or why not, and writes nothing. `restore --live --confirm-live` puts back a killed window's settings. `units --serial ID [--out DIR]` renders a systemd `--user` service (`KillMode=mixed`, `ExecStopPost=` restore) and a timer at the start time for the main checkout, and enables nothing. It never uses `locksettings`, never unlocks, wakes or taps, and infers no mode, geometry or timing: the jobs carry their own resolved profiles. |
| `apps/lab/test/test-overnight-window.py` | check | No-device fixture for the overnight window, in `test:unit:slow`, against a fake `adb` that keeps the phone's state and logs every call. Do Not Disturb: every window that runs a job sets heads-up off and DND to priority through `set_dnd` and reads both back, and restores both on every end; a `set_dnd` that fails or does not move `zen_mode` ends the window DND (exit 75) before any job with every setting restored; a DND found on is left on. The settings must be recorded, restored and read back on a normal end (including a job's brightness drift, capture it started, and the game left in front), on SIGINT to the process group twice mid-job, on a job failure through the real queue and the real setup (which must borrow the window's lease), and at the hard deadline against a child that ignores SIGINT and SIGTERM, closing before the window's end. LOCKED (bounded wait, queue note), IN_USE (an app, a call, mid-window), POWER, LEASE_BUSY, OUTSIDE_WINDOW and preflight must change nothing. A killed window's record must be recovered. The lease must be free after every window. The runner must never issue an unknown command, a tap, `locksettings`, or a write outside its setting list. Also checks the midnight-spanning window, the arming lead, and a deadline the host slept through. |
| `apps/lab/night-job.py run --job-id ID --game G --winner W --night N --label L --winner-sha256 SHA --plan-sha256 SHA\|none --budget-s S [--audio]` / `night-job.py title --game G [--recover]` | **device action** (the queue runs it, inside a window) | One queued night of a committed winner. Under the lease (borrowed from the window): custody and the queued sha256; for FNaF 2 a bundle emitted fresh into `artifacts/night-jobs/<job>/bundle`, its manifest and plan hashed into the record and refused if the plan moved since the job was queued or the checkout lacks the post-night static halt (669447b); the audio link where the runner reads audio (FNaF 4) or the job asked for it (`bt-audio-link.sh --ensure`); then the title, OBSERVED by SNAP (`native-frame.mjs`) and `title-observe.py` with the game's model, refused unless it offers the declared night (Custom Night for 7, 6th Night for 6, the digit under Continue for 1-5; FNaF 4 has no title model and FNaF 2 no Continue digit reader yet, so those refuse). The runner (`night-run.sh --live --confirm-live --no-grade` / `fnaf1-winner.mjs --live --confirm-live` / `fnaf4-run.sh --live --confirm-live --mode loop`: every runner is dry without the pair) shares its process group, takes the lease-held marker, and is bounded by `RUNNER_PRE_S` + the night + `RUNNER_POST_S`. After any end or abort the title is observed again, and if it is not, the game is force-stopped, relaunched and observed (mistake register 6). Exit 0 a night played to 6 AM or a death, 1 refused or failed, 75 a pre-night hold, 130 interrupted. Record: `artifacts/night-jobs/<job>/job.json` (frames under it by sha256). `title --recover` is the window's own recovery after a killed night. |
| `apps/lab/night_jobs.py` | internal module | The night job's closed vocabulary: `GAMES` (schema, package, title model, audio per game), `validate` (a `tools/device/*-winner.json` path, `custody` COMMITTED, the winner's own night), `resolve_binding` (fresh `emit.mjs`, plan `#observe-until`, hashes; detectors sha256 for the route winners), `budget_s` (each step's bound plus the night's length), `title_expectation`, `runner_command`, `run_id_from`, `terminal_of`. |
| `apps/lab/test/test-night-job.py` | check | No-device fixture for night jobs, in `test:unit`: the real queue, window, night-job.py, emit of the committed k3 winner, title-observe.py with the real FNaF 2 model, the lease and the evidence pack builder, against `testdata/fake_phone.py`. Enqueue refuses a night the winner does not bind, another game's winner, a path outside `tools/device` and shell text; `cue.queue.run` never claims a night. A title without Custom Night refuses before any runner; a FNaF 2 story night refuses (Continue digit unreadable). SIGINT to the window's group mid-night, with a runner that does not reset, leaves the game at an observed title, the settings restored and the lease released; a night SIGKILLed after the graces is recovered by the window. A night whose budget does not fit is not started. A played night runs under the window's lease with the marker, and its pack is written in the morning and named in the summary line. |
