The Minus 7 loop

A camera flash freezes that room for 6.66 seconds, and every stallable animatronic passes through one of three rooms. Sweep those rooms every five seconds and nothing ever reaches you. Ten inputs, then breathe.

WHY IT NEVER LAPSES
FLASH @ 0 s — stun 6.66 s FLASH @ 5 s +1.66 s MARGIN

A flash stuns for 6.66 s; the next lands 5 s later. The overlap absorbs a late sweep — up to ~1.6 s — before anyone gets a move.
